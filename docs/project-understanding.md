1. Product Purpose
Consultant Gate is a platform that connect students and consultants with experienced professionals who offer consultation sessions

The platform helps clients find consultants based on thier expertise 
review thier profiles , availability , and book consultations

consultants can also manage thier profiles , availability , and sessions

Administrators review consultants and handle operational issues

2. User Roles

: Client 
- Search for consultants based on their expertise.

- View consultant profiles and availability.

- Select a suitable consultation time.

- Book and attend consultations.


: Consultant

- Create and maintain a professional profile.

- Offer different consultation session types.

- Publish and manage availability.

- Manage consultation sessions.

: Administrator

- Review and approve consultants.

- Handle platform issues.

- Support platform operations.


Example Booking Journey

- A junior engineer wants guidance about starting a backend development career.

- The client opens Consultant Gate.

- The client searches for a consultant with backend development experience.

- The client reviews the consultant's profile and expertise.

- The client checks available consultation times.

- The client selects a suitable time and completes the booking.

- The client attends the consultation using the provided meeting details.
